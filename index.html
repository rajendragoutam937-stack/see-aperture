ABOUT THE PLATFORM

What is Aperture Vision?

Aperture Vision is an AI visual assistant. Use your camera or upload a picture to detect objects and ask questions about what is visible.

Live vision

Object detection runs continuously in your browser while the camera is active. When you ask a question, the current camera frame is sent to Groq Vision for visual understanding.

Image understanding

Upload a picture to detect common objects and ask the AI assistant about its contents.

AI responses and object detection may be inaccurate.
